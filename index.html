Hunbun,

Happy 21st birthday. I wish I could be there to say it in person, but for now this little page will have to carry it for me.

It's funny to think this all started with a random click on Omegle in February last year. Out of everyone, I found you, and somehow we never stopped talking.

You say you're emotionless. I don't believe you. I see it in how kind you are, in how you tell me when something doesn't sit right with you, and in how you cheer me on every time I come to you with a new idea or goal. That encouragement means more than you know.

I know the distance makes things complicated, and I know we're still waiting for the day we finally meet. Thank you for being patient with me, and for being willing to take the risk. I'm really glad it's you.

I hope 21 is soft and good to you. I'll be here cheering, the way you always do for me.

Yours,
